Dakiya

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Dakiya is a personal app used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google account. No one else can sign in to it.

What Dakiya reads

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): for new email in the inbox — the sender, the subject, Gmail's short preview, and whether it is unread. For a few filing rules it also reads the email's text to check the sender.
  • Its owner's own Apps Script project, read-only (script.projects.readonly): the email-filing rules the owner wrote, so that email those rules file away does not cause an alert.

Dakiya cannot send, delete, label, move or change any email, and it reads nothing else in the Google account.

How it is used

Only to decide whether a new email should cause an alert on the owner's iPhone, and to remove that alert once the email has been read, filed or deleted.

Where it goes

  • Everything is processed on the owner's own Mac at home.
  • An alert's sender, subject and short preview are delivered to the owner's iPhone through Apple Push Notification service.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere else. No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. There are no analytics or trackers.

What is kept

  • On that Mac: the sender and subject of recent emails (the latest 500) for the app's status screen, and the Google sign-in, readable only by the owner's user account.
  • On the iPhone: the alerts themselves, until they are cleared.

Limited Use

Dakiya's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting Dakiya's folder on the Mac removes everything it kept.

Contact

Through the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Dakiya.

Dakiya

Gmail alerts that clear themselves

Dakiya (Hindi for "postman") shows an alert on its owner's iPhone when new email arrives, and removes it once that email has been read anywhere.

A personal app. Dakiya is built for, and used only by, its developer. It is not offered to the public and cannot be downloaded.

It reads its owner's Gmail read-only, on the owner's own Mac at home, and sends the alerts to the owner's own iPhone. It cannot send, delete or change any email.
